4-Pillar Evaluation Methodology

ProcureIQ scores suppliers using an empirical, non-subjective multi-pillar algorithm:
• Delivery Reliability (35%): Evaluates historical on-time fulfillment.
• Product Quality (25%): Receiving dock defect-free pass rate.
• Price Competitiveness (25%): Price variance vs category index.
• Regulatory Compliance (15%): Valid ISO, insurance, and ESG audit scores.
